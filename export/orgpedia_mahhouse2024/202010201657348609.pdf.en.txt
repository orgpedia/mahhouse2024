The Proposal: -
The government was considering purchasing new computers and printers as the number of computers and printers was not sufficient for office work in the housing department.
At present, as per Government Policy, Department of Industries, Power and Labour, Government Decision No. Narrow-2016 / P.No.215 / Industries-4, D. As on 24th August, 2017, it has been made mandatory for all Government departments to purchase goods and services through the GovernmentMate-E-Marketplace (GeM) (https://gem.gov.in) portal developed under the National Procurement Policy of the Government of India for saving, supply of quality goods, fair price and transparency in the entire procurement process during the procurement period. Accordingly, computers and MEX materials have been procured as per the prescribed procedure and the government was considering sanctioning the cost and provision for such procurement.
Government Decision: - 1. Scanners with 15 computers and 10 printers have been procured for the Housing Department and these purchases have been made through the Government-E-Marketplace (GeM) (https://gem.gov.in) portal as per the prevailing policy of the Government. The purchase amount of the department is Rs. The lowest bidder in online bidding (Lowest!) by adopting the online bidding method, with more than 3. 00 lakhs. Computers and printer materials have been purchased from suppliers Perfect House Private Limited, MUMBAI-400 034 and ICON Infotech Private Limited, MUMBAI-400 007. For this, Rs. 12,77,485 - (Rs. 12 lakh seventy seven thousand four hundred TAA only) is being approved for expenditure and grant. The details of the purchase are as follows: -
